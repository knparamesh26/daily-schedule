Finalize Brand Identity Guidelines for Global Launch

description Description

The objective of this task is to consolidate all visual assets, typography rules, and color palettes into a comprehensive brand book for the upcoming Q4 global launch. This document will serve as the single source of truth for all creative agencies and internal marketing teams.

Key Deliverables:

  • Finalize the primary and secondary color hex codes for digital and print.
  • Document spacing rules for the logo lockup across different screen sizes.
  • Define the tone of voice and linguistic standards for international localization.
  • Export all high-resolution logo variants in SVG and PNG formats.
